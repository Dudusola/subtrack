Subtrack

Privacy

Subtrack is free, open-source software. This page says what it reads, what it keeps and how to remove it. It applies to any copy of Subtrack, and the person running that copy is responsible for it.

What we store

  • Your email address and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password. Nobody can read the password itself.
  • The subscriptions you add or confirm: name, price, currency, billing cycle, date, category and whether it’s a free trial.
  • The results of your last inbox scan, until you confirm or dismiss them.
  • A record of which reminder emails were sent, so you don’t get the same one twice. These are deleted after 60 days.

Your email inbox

  • Read-only, only when you ask. Your mail is read only when you start a scan. It searches for billing emails (receipts, invoices, renewals) from the last 13 months.
  • Nothing kept from the emails themselves. From each email it works out a merchant name, an amount, a date and a short subject line. The email text is not stored.
  • No lasting access. With Google sign-in, the access token is never saved and is revoked as soon as the scan ends. With an app password, the password is used for that one connection and never saved. You can also delete the app password from your email account at any time.
  • Google user data is used only to show you your own subscriptions. It is never sold or shared, and never used for ads or for training AI models. Subtrack’s use of data from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Email we send you

Reminders before a charge or a trial ends, if you have them on (turn them off in Settings), and password reset links when you ask for one. No marketing.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. There’s no tracking or analytics, and no ads.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account, subscriptions, scan results and reminder history. Settings → Download backup gives you a copy first.

Questions
