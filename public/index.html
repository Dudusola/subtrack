Subtrack

Find every charge hiding in your inbox.

Connect Gmail and Subtrack reads your receipts, lists what you’re paying for, and puts each renewal in your calendar before it bills you.

  1. Reads receiptsSearches 13 months of billing mail with read-only access. The Google token is revoked the moment the scan ends.
  2. You confirmNothing is tracked until you tick it. Add anything the scan missed by hand.
  3. It reminds youA private calendar link alerts your phone 1, 3 or 7 days before each charge.

Open source under the MIT license. Your data stays on the server you run it on.
